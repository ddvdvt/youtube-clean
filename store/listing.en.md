## Detailed description

You open one video and an hour later you are deep in a feed of short clips. Video Declutter removes what pulls you in and keeps what you came for: search, subscriptions and the video itself. Works on YouTube in Chrome and Edge (desktop).

Three modes, one click

• Minimal: no Shorts, games, Explore section or ad slots in the interface. Recommendations stay.
• Focus: also no home recommendations, no "Up next" column, no end screens and no autoplay.
• Subscriptions only: like Focus, and the home page opens your subscriptions.

Every item has its own switch

• Shorts everywhere: shelves, menu entry, channel tab, Shorts in search. Optionally open Shorts links in the normal player — no endless feed.
• Home recommendations: a calm empty page instead of the feed.
• The "Up next" column, cards over the video, the end video wall and the fullscreen grid.
• Autoplay is switched off for every new video. Turn it back on yourself and the extension leaves it alone.
• Comments and live chat — if you want.
• Ad slots in the interface — cosmetic only: no requests are blocked and in-player ads are not touched.

Focus schedule and a reminder

• On chosen days and hours the strict mode turns itself on and off.
• A gentle reminder after a daily number of minutes. The counter stays on your device.

Fixed fast

The site changes its markup often. All rules live in one validated data file, and the extension notices when a rule stops matching and tells you in its popup. Optionally turn on daily rule updates: only a signed data file is downloaded, never code.

Privacy

No analytics, no ads, no account. What you watch and search is never sent anywhere. No network requests by default. Access to www.youtube.com only.
